NORTH ATLANTIC OCEAN

THE FAROE
ISLANDS

A LAND SHAPED BY WIND AND WATER.

The Faroe Islands are a remote archipelago situated between Iceland, Norway and Scotland. Known for their dramatic landscapes, the islands feature steep coastal cliffs, green mountains, waterfalls and small settlements overlooking the Atlantic Ocean.

The changing weather gives the landscape a constantly evolving appearance, making every view feel different.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

  • Múlafossur Waterfall — A spectacular waterfall flowing directly into the Atlantic Ocean.
  • Sørvágsvatn — A famous lake that appears to float above the ocean because of an optical illusion.
  • Gásadalur — A peaceful village surrounded by dramatic mountain scenery.
  • Drangarnir — Two remarkable sea stacks rising from the ocean.
BEST TIME TO VISIT MAY – SEPTEMBER
EAST ASIA

KYOTO,
JAPAN

WHERE TRADITION MEETS TIMELESS BEAUTY.

Kyoto served as Japan's imperial capital for more than a thousand years. Its historic temples, traditional wooden houses, peaceful gardens and cultural districts preserve an important part of Japanese heritage.

The city is particularly beautiful during cherry blossom season and autumn, when its landscapes transform with seasonal colours.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

  • Fushimi Inari Shrine — Famous for its thousands of red torii gates.
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove — A remarkable pathway surrounded by towering bamboo.
  • Kinkaku-ji — The famous Golden Pavilion reflected in a peaceful pond.
  • Gion — A historic district known for traditional architecture.
BEST TIME TO VISIT MARCH – MAY / OCTOBER – NOVEMBER
NORTH AFRICA

MOROCCO

A JOURNEY THROUGH COLOUR AND HISTORY.

Morocco is a country of remarkable contrasts, where ancient cities, colourful marketplaces, mountain villages and vast desert landscapes exist within one fascinating destination.

Its architecture, craftsmanship, cuisine and traditions reflect a rich mixture of Amazigh, Arab, African and Mediterranean influences.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

  • Chefchaouen — Famous for its beautiful blue-painted streets.
  • Marrakech — A vibrant city filled with historic architecture and markets.
  • Sahara Desert — Vast dunes and extraordinary desert landscapes.
  • Aït Benhaddou — A historic fortified settlement known for its traditional earthen architecture.
BEST TIME TO VISIT MARCH – MAY / SEPTEMBER – NOVEMBER
GILGIT-BALTISTAN, SOUTH ASIA

HUNZA
VALLEY

WHERE THE MOUNTAINS TOUCH THE SKY.

Located in northern Pakistan, Hunza Valley is surrounded by spectacular mountain ranges, including peaks of the Karakoram. Its turquoise lakes, historic forts, glaciers and welcoming communities make it an extraordinary destination.

The valley is also known for its cultural heritage and traditional settlements.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

  • Attabad Lake — Famous for its striking turquoise water.
  • Passu Cones — A dramatic collection of pointed mountain peaks.
  • Baltit Fort — A historic fort overlooking Karimabad.
  • Altit Fort — An ancient architectural landmark with a long history.
  • Khunjerab Pass — A high-altitude mountain pass connecting Pakistan and China.
BEST TIME TO VISIT MAY – OCTOBER
EUROPE AND ASIA

ISTANBUL,
TÜRKIYE

WHERE TWO CONTINENTS MEET.

Istanbul is a city where different continents, histories and cultural influences come together. Located on both sides of the Bosphorus Strait, it has served as an important centre of several historical empires.

Its mosques, palaces, bazaars and waterfront neighbourhoods create a distinctive urban atmosphere.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

  • Hagia Sophia — A remarkable architectural monument with a complex history.
  • Blue Mosque — Known for its impressive Ottoman architecture.
  • Grand Bazaar — One of the world's oldest and largest covered markets.
  • Galata Tower — A historic tower offering panoramic city views.
  • Bosphorus Strait — The waterway separating the European and Asian sides of the city.
BEST TIME TO VISIT APRIL – MAY / SEPTEMBER – OCTOBER
